## Description

Monitors HPE SimpliVity through its API: the status of hosts and virtual machines and the capacity, deduplication and compression of OmniStack clusters.

The plugin pack provides these checks:

* [Hosts status](#apps-virtualization-hpe-simplivity-restapi-hosts)
* [Omnistack Clusters](#apps-virtualization-hpe-simplivity-restapi-omnistack-clusters)
* [Virtual Machines status](#apps-virtualization-hpe-simplivity-restapi-virtual-machines)
