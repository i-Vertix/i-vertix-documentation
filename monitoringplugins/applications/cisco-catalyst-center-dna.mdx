## Description

Monitors Cisco Catalyst Center (DNA Center) through its REST API: the health of managed network devices, sites and connected clients.

The plugin pack provides these checks:

* [Network devices](#apps-cisco-dnac-restapi-network-devices)
* [Sites](#apps-cisco-dnac-restapi-sites)
