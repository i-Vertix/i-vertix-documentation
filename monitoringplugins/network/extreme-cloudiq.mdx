## Description

Monitors Extreme CloudIQ through its API: the health of devices, clients, WiFi, services and the network, plus device status, CPU, memory, WiFi interfaces and uptime.

The plugin pack provides these checks:

* [Client health](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-location-client-health)
* [CPU](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-device-cpu)
* [Device health](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-location-device-health)
* [Device status](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-device-status)
* [Memory](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-device-memory)
* [Network health](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-location-network-health)
* [Services health](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-location-services-health)
* [Uptime](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-device-uptime)
* [WiFi health](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-location-wifi-health)
* [WiFi interfaces](#network-extreme-cloudiq-restapi-device-wifi-interfaces)
